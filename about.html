About

This site is dedicated to my collection of various discontinued items, my list is always growing as I'm still learning about new items. I actively collect 1.0 items, ornates, discontinued furniture, discontinued event HQ food and event tokens. If you have something you think I'd be interested in or missed please reach out. If you see an item that's red, that means I don't think the item actually exists and is database only, but you never know. I'd like spares of everything but equipment so anything empty or I only have 1-2 I'd probably want. If you want to trade I'm open for that, if you just want gil I'm also open to negotiate!

This website is based off of Serena Falh's, another collector. Thanks for the assistance.
